Gullswick is small on purpose. Two rooms, both facing the water, above a shed where oysters have been graded and packed since the 1930s.

You will hear the shed. Work starts at half past five most mornings and it is not quiet. Guests who want to sleep past seven should probably stay somewhere else, and we would rather say so now than read about it later.

## What you get

Both rooms are plain — white walls, a good bed, a shower, a chair by the window. There is no television. There is a kettle, and there is a shelf of paperbacks left behind by previous guests, which is either charming or a fire risk depending on who you ask.

Breakfast is not included. There is a café on the corner that opens at seven and does the best sausage sandwich in Kent, and we are not going to compete with that.

## Parking

There is none. The public car park on Gorrell Tank is a two-minute walk and costs £8 a day. In July and August it fills by nine in the morning.
