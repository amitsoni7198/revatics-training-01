Hastings still lands fish off the beach. There is no harbour, so the boats are winched up the shingle on the Stade, and the tall black net shops behind them have been there in one form or another since the sixteenth century.

The house is a minute from all of it. That is the entire pitch.

## The market

The fish market on Rock-a-Nore sells whatever came in that morning. It opens when the boats are unloaded, which is not a fixed time — usually between seven and nine, earlier in summer. Bring cash and a bag.

If you want somebody to cook it for you, there are three places on the same road that will do exactly that.

## Parking

There is none at the house and the Old Town is a permit zone. We can issue a visitor permit for the length of your stay if you tell us the registration in advance, but the streets are medieval and the spaces are small. Most guests park at Rock-a-Nore and walk.
