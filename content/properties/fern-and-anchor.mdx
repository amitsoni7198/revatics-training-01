The garden is the reason people come back. It runs in five terraces down the hill and joins the coast path at the bottom through a gate that is ours and that we leave unlocked.

Nobody planned it. The terraces were dug for vegetables during the war and have been slowly reclaimed by something closer to a wild garden — valerian, fennel, sea kale, and a fig that should not survive the winter but does.

## Fossils

Lyme is what it is. There is a box in the hall with hammers, a chisel, three hand lenses and a laminated card explaining what you are allowed to take from the beach and what you are not.

> The short version: loose on the shingle is fine, in the cliff is not, and never go under an overhang after rain.

Low tide is the only time worth going. Ask at breakfast and we will tell you when it is.

## The hill

It is steep. From the Cobb it is about eight minutes up, and most of that is at a gradient that surprises people. There is parking at the house, which is the practical answer.
