This piece is not finished. Several of the dates below are unconfirmed and at least two are wrong.

## Regattas

Salcombe is the second week of August, as always. Whitstable Oyster Festival is the last full week of July. Hastings Old Town Carnival is the first Saturday in August, weather permitting, which in 2024 it was not.

## Open days

The lifeboat station open day is a Sunday in September — the date is set in June and we will update this when it is.

## Food

The Rye scallop festival is in February, so not this piece. The Whitstable oyster landing ceremony is in July.
