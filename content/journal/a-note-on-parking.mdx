Three of our six properties have parking. Three do not, and two of those are in medieval street patterns where parking was never going to happen.

## Where there is parking

The Salt House, The Old Coastguard and Fern & Anchor all have spaces, included in the rate. Thorne Barn has more space than it will ever need.

## Where there is not

Gullswick and The Herring House. At Gullswick the public car park is two minutes away and costs £8 a day, and it fills by nine in July and August.

At The Herring House the Old Town is permit-only. We can issue a visitor permit if you send us the registration before you arrive — but the streets are narrow, the spaces are short, and most guests give up and use Rock-a-Nore.

If parking matters to you, book one of the first three.
