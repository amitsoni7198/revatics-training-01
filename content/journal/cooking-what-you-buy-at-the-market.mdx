If you are staying somewhere with a kitchenette, or you have a barbecue and a beach, this is the entire technique.

## Mackerel

Very hot pan or charcoal. Four minutes a side. Salt, lemon. Nothing else.

## Plaice

Whole, on the bone, in a wide pan with butter. Eight to ten minutes depending on size, spooning the butter over. Parsley if you have it.

## Herring

Split, floured, fried in five minutes. Traditionally with oatmeal, which is Scottish rather than local but is better.

## Skate

The one people avoid. Wing, in a pan with butter until it starts to catch, then capers and a splash of vinegar off the heat. Fifteen minutes and it is the best thing on this list.

Ask the fishmonger to prepare any of these. They will do it in about forty seconds and it will be better than your attempt.
