No preamble. These work.

1. **The fishermen's museum, Hastings.** Free, small, contains an entire boat indoors. Forty minutes, more if they climb into it.
2. **The miniature railway from Hythe.** It runs in the rain. The carriages are enclosed. It is thirteen miles and the driver waves.
3. **Rock pooling at low tide.** Rain does not matter if they are already wet. Bring a bucket.
4. **The castle at Rye.** A tower with stairs and a view, which is enough.
5. **The bird hides at Rye Harbour.** Dry, quiet, binoculars. Works better than expected for children who like counting things.
6. **The fossil shops in Lyme.** Free to browse, and a £2 ammonite is a souvenir that survives the journey home.
7. **The pier at Hastings.** Wide, flat, exposed, and there is something about walking out over water in the rain that children find genuinely funny.

If none of it works, the answer is the shelf of paperbacks in the hall and an early bath.
