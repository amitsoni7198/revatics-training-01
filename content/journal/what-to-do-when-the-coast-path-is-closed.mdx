The South West Coast Path and the sections along the Sussex cliffs close more often than visitors expect. A cliff fall after heavy rain, or a red warning for wind, and a stretch is shut for a week or a season.

This is not a disaster. It is just a change of plan.

## Indoors

Every town on this coast has a small museum that is better than it has any right to be. Hastings has the fishermen's museum in an old chapel on the Stade. Lyme has the fossil collection. Rye has an entire castle in the middle of the town that most people walk past.

## Still outdoors, but sheltered

The marshes behind the coast are flat, low and far less exposed. Rye Harbour reserve has four hides, and sitting in a hide in horizontal rain watching a marsh harrier work a reed bed is a better afternoon than it sounds.

## The honest option

Find a pub with a fire, take the paperback from the hall shelf, and stop trying to make the day into something. The weather will change. It always does, usually within about six hours.
