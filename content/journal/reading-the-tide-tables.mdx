Almost everything worth doing on this coast depends on the tide, and most visitors never look at a table until they are cut off by one.

## The basics

There are two high tides and two low tides in roughly twenty-five hours. Each one falls about fifty minutes later than the equivalent tide the day before. If low water is at nine this morning, it will be around ten tomorrow.

## Spring and neap

Spring tides — nothing to do with the season — happen around the new and full moon, and they are the biggest. More beach at low water, less at high. Neaps, at the quarter moons, are the opposite and much less dramatic.

For fossils, rock pools and the wider beach walks, you want a spring low.

## Where to get them

There is a table on the noticeboard in the hall at every one of our properties, and it is checked weekly. The apps are fine but they are not always corrected for the local port, and the difference between Dover and here is about half an hour.
