Breakfast is included at four of the six properties and served between eight and half past nine.

## Where it comes from

Bread from the bakery on Lion Street, which mills some of its own flour. Eggs from a smallholding at Playden — about four miles, and the yolks are the colour that surprises people. Bacon and sausage from a butcher in Northiam. Milk from a dairy at Icklesham.

None of this is a sustainability position. It is that the food is better and the suppliers are down the road.

## Why it stops at 09:30

Because one person cooks it, and that person also strips the rooms. There is no kitchen brigade behind a door. If you need to leave earlier, tell us the night before and there will be something on the table.

## Dietary requirements

Tell us when you book rather than at the table. We can do most things with notice and very little without it.
