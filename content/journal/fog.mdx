Sea fog here is not weather so much as an event. It arrives in the early morning when warm air sits over cold water, and it can take a headland from clear to invisible in the time it takes to walk down to the beach.

It is worth getting up for. Sound behaves oddly — the foghorn at Dungeness carries fifteen miles on the right morning — and the light, for about an hour after it starts to lift, is the reason photographers keep coming back to this stretch of coast.

If you are walking the cliff path, do not. Come down and walk the shingle instead.
