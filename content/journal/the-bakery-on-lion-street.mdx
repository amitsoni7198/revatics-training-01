We buy the bread for breakfast here and have done since 2011.

They mill some of their own flour, which is unusual, and they bake through the night rather than proving overnight and baking at five, which is more unusual still. The sourdough is the thing, but the morning buns are what people come back for.

Get there before nine on a Saturday. By eleven there is not much left, and by half past there is a sign on the door.

Closed Mondays. Closed the first two weeks of February, every year, without exception.
