Mackerel shoal close to shore through late summer, and for a few weeks they are so abundant that catching them is less a skill than an inevitability.

A set of feathers, a weight, and a hand line will do. Drop it, let it sink, pull it up in jerks. If they are there, you will know within a minute.

## Killing them properly

If you catch one, kill it immediately and put it on ice. Mackerel goes from superb to inedible faster than any fish in these waters — a few hours in the sun and it is finished. This is the entire reason mackerel has a poor reputation inland.

## Cooking it

Fifteen minutes over charcoal, or four minutes a side in a very hot pan. Salt, lemon. Nothing else is required and most of what people add makes it worse.
