It started with three houses and a shared advert in a magazine that no longer exists.

The idea was straightforward. Independent guesthouses were being squeezed by listing sites taking twenty per cent of every booking, and none of us individually had the traffic to do anything about it. Together, we might.

## What we actually share

A booking system. A phone line that rolls over when one house cannot answer. Suppliers, which means better prices on everything from bread to bed linen. And a dinner four times a year at which the argument about the best view happens.

## What we do not share

Ownership, style, prices, or opinions. The six houses have almost nothing in common beyond geography and a general view that a guesthouse should feel like somebody's house.

## Fifteen years

Two of the original three are still in. The logo argument lasted nine months and was settled by a vote that two people still describe as procedurally irregular.
