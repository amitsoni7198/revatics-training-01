The English Channel is at its warmest in September and October, not in high summer. Water lags air by about two months, which means the first week of October frequently offers better swimming than the first week of June.

It also means an empty beach.

## Practical

Bring a changing robe or a very large towel. The wind after you get out is the problem, not the water. Neoprene socks make more difference than gloves.

Do not swim alone off the shingle when there is any swell — the beach shelves steeply on this coast and the undertow is real. Ask us where is safe on the day.
