Midwinter on this coast is the season nobody books and the one several of us would choose.

The light between two and four in the afternoon is extraordinary — low, long and orange — and there is nobody on the beach to share it with. The sea is at its warmest relative to the air, which is why the fog comes, and why the whole thing occasionally looks like something out of a film.

> Four in the afternoon in December here is worth more than any hour in July.

Bring a coat that actually works. The wind comes off the water and there is nothing between here and France to slow it down.
