In November we wrote about the bird log at Thorne Barn and the unconfirmed hoopoe of 2019.

A guest who stayed in the Granary room in January wrote to us afterwards. She had been in the same room in September 2019, she had made the entry, and she had photographs.

They are not good photographs. They were taken on a phone through a window at some distance. But the crest is unmistakable and so is the barring on the wing.

## So

It was a hoopoe. They turn up on the south coast most years, usually in spring, occasionally in autumn on their way back.

The log has been annotated. The original entry stands, in capitals, as written.
