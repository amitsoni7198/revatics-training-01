There is a hardback notebook on the hall table at Thorne Barn. Guests write down what they have seen. It started in 2011 and is now on its fourth volume.

Most of it is what you would expect — marsh harrier, avocet, little egret, the terns in season. Some of it is aspirational. One entry from 2019 reads, in confident capitals, HOOPOE, followed by a time and no further detail.

Nobody has ever been able to confirm it. It is possible; they turn up on the south coast most years. It is also possible that somebody saw a green woodpecker at distance and had a very good afternoon.

The log stays. So does the hoopoe.
