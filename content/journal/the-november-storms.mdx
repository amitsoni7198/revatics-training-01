Three days after the last piece went up, the first named storm of the season came through and took about four metres off the beach at Camber.

It will come back. That is what the shingle does.

## Watching safely

From the top of the town, from behind a wall, from inside. Not from the sea wall, not on the pier, and not on the shingle, where waves on this coast regularly run twenty metres further up the beach than the previous one did.

People die doing this every winter and they are almost always people who were standing somewhere that had been fine for the last ten minutes.

## Afterwards

The morning after a big storm is the best fossil hunting and the best beachcombing of the year. Go at low water. Take a bag.
