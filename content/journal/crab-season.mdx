Brown crab is landed along this coast most of the year, but there is a stretch between late June and early August when it is heavy, sweet and cheap all at once.

The rest of the time it is fine. In those six weeks it is worth going out of your way for.

## Buying it

Ask for hen crabs if you want the brown meat and cocks if you want the white. If the fishmonger looks at you strangely for knowing this, you have found a good one.

A dressed crab from the market at Hastings will cost about half what the same crab costs on a plate two hundred metres away, and it takes four minutes to eat on the shingle with a plastic fork.
