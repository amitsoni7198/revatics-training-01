Waves rarely arrive straight on. They come at an angle, push shingle up the beach at that angle, and gravity pulls it straight back down. Repeat a few million times and the entire beach travels along the coast.

On this stretch it moves east at roughly a metre a day.

## Why the towns moved

Rye was a port. It is now two miles inland, because the shingle kept coming and the harbour silted up. Winchelsea was destroyed by the sea and rebuilt on a hill. New Romney's harbour was buried in a single storm in 1287.

This coastline has never been stable and is not now.

## The groynes

Those wooden fences running down the beach are there to interrupt the drift and hold shingle in place. You can see them working — the beach is noticeably higher on the western side of each one.

Dungeness exists because the drift had to put all that shingle somewhere.
