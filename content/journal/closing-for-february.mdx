All six properties close for the same two weeks every February. Guests occasionally ask why we would shut in a month when we could take bookings.

## The work

Rooms get repainted on a rotation. Floors get sanded. The boiler at The Salt House gets a service that takes two days and involves no hot water. Mattresses get turned and, every few years, replaced.

None of this can happen with people in the building, and doing it badly around guests produces exactly the sort of stay nobody wants.

## The other reason

The people who run these houses have been working since the middle of March. Six of the eleven staff across the collective have been here more than five years, which does not happen if you never let anyone stop.

We reopen at the start of March. The dates are on each property page.
