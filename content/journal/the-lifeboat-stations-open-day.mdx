The station opens to the public one Sunday in September. There is a launch demonstration at eleven and again at two, and between the two you can walk through the boathouse and climb into the boat.

## What actually happens

The launch is the point. The carriage is driven into the shallows by a tractor, the boat comes off, and the whole thing takes about ninety seconds once it starts. Stand on the shingle to the east — the crowd goes west and the view is worse.

## The rest of it

There is a tea urn, a raffle and a table of second-hand paperbacks. The crew answer questions patiently, including the ones they must have been asked several thousand times.

It is free. There is a bucket. Put something in it.
