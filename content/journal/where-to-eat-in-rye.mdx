Rye has more restaurants per head than a town of four thousand people can reasonably support, which means the standard is high and the turnover is brutal.

These have all been open more than five years.

## The reliable ones

The fish restaurant on the high street is the obvious choice and deserves to be. Book. The pub on Mermaid Street does one thing — a pie — and does it extremely well.

The place on Lion Street with six tables takes no bookings and opens at six. If there is a queue at ten to, join it.

## Winter only

There is a small Italian place near the church that is wonderful in February and unbearable in August, when it takes twice as many covers as the kitchen can handle. We are not going to name it. Ask us when you arrive and we will tell you which one it is.
