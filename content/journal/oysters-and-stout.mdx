The native oyster season runs from September to April — the months with an R in them, which is the old rule and, unusually for old rules, roughly correct. Natives spawn in summer and are poor eating when they do.

Rock oysters are available year-round and are perfectly good. Natives are better, more expensive, and worth having once.

## The pairing

Stout and oysters is not a marketing invention. Both were cheap food in nineteenth-century port towns and they happen to work: the roast bitterness cuts the salt, and the texture contrast is the whole point.

Champagne also works and costs eight times as much.

## Where

The oyster company on the harbour does them at a counter with no ceremony whatsoever. Half a dozen, a wedge of lemon, a glass of something dark, standing up, in November, in the wind. Better than most restaurants manage sitting down.
